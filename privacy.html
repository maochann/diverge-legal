Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Diverge ("the app", "we") is a small social app made by an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash), a username, a display name, and an optional bio and profile photo.
  • Content you create: posts, photos attached to posts, comments and likes.
  • Your choices: which accounts you follow, mute or block, and the feed mode you pick.
  • Reports: if you report a post or an account, we store the report, your account id and any details you type.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers or analytics about how long you look at things. The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your profile and posts to other members, and build your feed from the accounts you chose to follow.
  • To keep the community safe: act on reports, and enforce blocks and mutes.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Your feed is built only from your own explicit choices (who you follow, mute or block, and the feed mode you pick), never from inferred interests.

Who can see what

Diverge is a members-only app. Your profile, posts, comments and likes are visible to other signed-in members, except people you have blocked or who have blocked you. Your lists of followed, muted and blocked accounts are private to you. Nobody can see who follows you, and there are no follower counts.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) on servers operated by Supabase, Inc. and its cloud providers. Data may be processed outside the country you live in.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, mutes, blocks and reports you filed are deleted. Server logs are kept only briefly.

Your controls

  • Edit your profile at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Delete any post or comment you made.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Edit profile → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Block, mute or report any account from its profile or from the menu on a post.

Children

Diverge is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and note the change in the app.
