Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Diverge ("the app", "we") is a small social app made by an individual developer. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash), a username, a display name, and an optional bio and profile photo.
  • Content you create: posts, photos attached to posts, comments and likes.
  • Your choices: which accounts you follow, mute or block, and the feed mode you pick.
  • Reports: if you report a post or an account, we store the report, your account id and any details you type.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) kept briefly by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • Daily prompt reminder (optional): only if you turn the reminder on in Settings, we store a push notification token for your device, your device's time zone, your app language and the time of day you chose. We use these for one purpose: to send you one notification a day with the day's prompt. Turning the reminder off, or deleting your account, removes this. We send no other notifications.
  • App language: if you choose a language in Settings, that choice is stored on your device.

We do not collect your contacts, precise location, advertising identifiers or analytics about how long you look at things. The app contains no advertising and no third-party tracking. The push notification token described above is the only device-level identifier we hold, and only while your reminder is on.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your profile and posts to other members, and build your feed from the accounts you chose to follow.
  • To keep the community safe: act on reports, and enforce blocks and mutes.
  • To fix problems and keep the service secure.

We never sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising. Your feed is built only from your own explicit choices (who you follow, mute or block, and the feed mode you pick), never from inferred interests.

Who can see what

Diverge is a members-only app. Your profile, posts, comments, replies and likes are visible to other signed-in members, except people you have blocked or who have blocked you. If someone branches ("forks") a new post from one of yours, that post shows your username as its origin for as long as your post is visible to the reader; if you delete your post, the link is removed. Posts that answer the day's prompt show which prompt they answered. Your lists of followed, muted and blocked accounts are private to you. Nobody can see who follows you, and there are no follower counts.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database, authentication and file storage) on servers operated by Supabase, Inc. and its cloud providers. Data may be processed outside the country you live in.

Two features send limited data to other providers only when you use them: tapping "See translation" on a post sends that post's text to DeepL SE to produce a translation (the result is cached so each post is translated at most once per language), and the daily prompt reminder is delivered through Expo's push notification service (Expo, Inc.) and Apple's notification service, which receive your device token and the text of the day's prompt. Neither is given your account details.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos, comments, replies, likes, follows, mutes, blocks, reminder settings (including the push token) and reports you filed are deleted. Posts that other people branched from yours stay theirs; only the link to your post is removed. Server logs are kept only briefly.

Your controls

  • Edit your profile at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Delete any post or comment you made.
  • Delete your account from Settings → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent.
  • Turn the daily prompt reminder on or off at any time from Settings → Daily prompt reminder. It is off unless you turn it on.
  • Choose the app language from Settings → Language. This never changes other people's posts.
  • Block, mute or report any account from its profile or from the menu on a post.

Children

Diverge is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and note the change in the app.
